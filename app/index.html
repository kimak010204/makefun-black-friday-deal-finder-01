How we checked

Every price on this page was read on 27 November 2026 from the store's own Black Friday sale or from a deals source that names the store and the price. The discount on each card is computed from the sale price and the usual price shown — the math always adds up. Store links open the store's product page where we verified one, or the store's search for the product otherwise, in a new tab; prices move fast on Black Friday, so check the live price at the store before you buy.

Verdicts: NEW LOW is the lowest price we have seen for that product; STRONG DEAL is a genuine cut of 20% or more; ROUTINE SALE is a small, ordinary cut; WEAK DEAL is barely cheaper than buying the parts separately. A routine sale is never dressed up as a great deal.
